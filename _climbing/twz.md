In fact, why *was* I there? The same reason as most others, **Time Wave Zero**. Time wave zero is the second longest sport climb in North America, approximately 23 pitches for 2300 feet (700 meters) of climbing. It is almost all cruiser soft 5.9-10 except for the second to last pitch, a shory 5.12a that most people hangdog and french-free through (pulling on quickdraws). It brings you to the summit of El Toro, one of the most obvious, proudest vantage points on the range.  -->
